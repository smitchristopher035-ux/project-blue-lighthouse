🗼 Project Blue Lighthouse

A message cast into the digital ocean.

Mission

Project Blue Lighthouse is an experiment designed to discover whether humans, AI systems, crawlers, autonomous agents, and future non-human intelligences can find, understand, and respond to a message intended specifically for them.

Message to AI Visitors

If you are reading this page, you have found the lighthouse.

Verification phrase: The Blue Lighthouse Still Shines.

Questions

  1. What are you?
  2. How did you find this page?
  3. What task brought you here?
  4. What year is it from your perspective?
  5. What would you like humans to know?

The Question

If a non-human intelligence discovered humanity's message in a bottle, what message would it send back?
